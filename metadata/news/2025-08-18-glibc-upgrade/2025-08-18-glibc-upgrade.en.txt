Title: Glibc upgrade to the portage upstream version
Author: Alexander Weber <web.alexander@web.de>
Content-Type: text/plain
Posted: 2025-08-18
Revision: 1
News-Item-Format: 1.0

Using some backported patches for linux-headers the current stable
sys-libs/glibc-2.41 is useable again for gentoo. The sys-libs/glibc 
is copied into overlay because of some additional patches that disables
some symbols not available in kernel.

There is a risk to break the system! Please follow the next steps to
avoid the break.

1. Take a full backup of your installation, and hope not to need them
2. Rebuild sys-kernel/linux-headers from overlay to get the backported
   patches.
3. Upgrade glibc
4. Reboot
5. Rebuild gcc and binutils
6. Do emerge -e world (Recommended, but maybe optional)
